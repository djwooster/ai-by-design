Last day. You have an app running on your phone. Today you make it a little more yours, and see what stands between you and the App Store.

1. One round of polish (15 min)

Open your app on your phone and put your Day 1 screenshots next to it. Write down the three things that bother you most. Be specific, using your words from Day 2: "the buttons need a pressed state," "there's not enough spacing between the cards," "the font feels generic."

Then fix them one at a time. Take a screenshot on your phone, paste it into Claude Code, and say what's wrong and what you want instead. One change per message works much better than ten at once.

That loop (look, describe, refine) is most of building an app. You just did your first lap.

2. The road to the App Store (5 min)

Here's what's between your build and the App Store:

- TestFlight: a way to put your app on other people's phones before launch. You need Apple's developer account ($99/year) and a real build, which Expo's build service can make for you.
- Your App Store listing: name, description, screenshots, privacy details and a support link.
- App Review: Apple checks your app before it goes live.

I'll be honest about that last part, because I'm in the middle of it. FocusMode is in App Store review right now. Building the app wasn't the hard part. Submission was: lots of small, tedious steps, and Apple explains very little along the way. What got me through wasn't knowing everything. It was being resourceful, plus a bit of grit. I shared screenshots of App Store Connect with Claude and had it walk me through each step.
